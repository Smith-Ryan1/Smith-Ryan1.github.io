## Billing And Economics
Three main factors of cost


|Item|factor|
|----|----|
|Compute| Charged per hour/second (second only for linux), varies by instance type|
|Storage| Typically charged per GB|
|Data Transfer| Outbound data is aggregated and charged, Inbound has no charge with certain exceptions, charge is typically based on the GB of the transfer.|

You pay AWS for what you use, at a discount as you use more services, and you pay less when you reserve services. 

There are custom pricing options available if the model of pricing AWS offers does not meet the needs of the business.

TCO or Total cost of ownership is the financial estimate of indirect and direct costs to run a system. These costs can include server costs, maintinance costs, storage costs, and network costs. 

AWS offers a pricing calculator
