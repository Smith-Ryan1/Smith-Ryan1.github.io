Networks exist in daily life, if you are connected to any sort of internet, be it at home, school, work, or through your tellcommunication provider cellular data plan, if you are connected to others, you are part of the network. Being on the network, you can be affected in numerous ways, through nefarious websites collecting data you did not know you signed up to collect, or willingly giving away data that you could regret later. It is important to be educated on the ramifications of being a part of a interconnected system like the internet, and yet most people are blissfully unaware of what could happen.
