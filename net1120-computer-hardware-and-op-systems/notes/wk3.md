Expansion cards are expansions of your motherboard allowing for numerous additions such as more USB slots, space for graphics cards, space for audio cards, and so on. 

HDDs hard disk drives are spinning magnetic disks that rely on how fast the disk spins to be how fast the computer can read the data on the disks, its open and many parts make it more suseptible to damage while being a cheaper alternative to SSDs

SSDs solid state drives, are solid blocks of flash data sender and recievers. This flash data is faster than the disk and produces less heat and requires less power. SSDs are also more sturdy than HDDs as they are, again, solid blocks. They come at a cost of being more expensive.
