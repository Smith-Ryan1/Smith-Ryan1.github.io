Knowledge in Computer Hardware, and the operating system you are running on, are probably more important than knowing how to do Cybersecurity as a whole. If you know what your system is capable of, the ins and the outs, it will be a far more useful tool, and not to mention just plain easier to do your job, than if you did not know a thing about what you are working with. It could also prove to be useful knowing different operating systems in an office IT setting to provide support when someones system is acting like it should not be. It is a lot easier to be helpful when you know what you are doing in the first place.
