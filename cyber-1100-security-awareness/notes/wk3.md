|Types of malware|Description|
|-----|-----|
|spyware| designed to track and log what you do, you know, like a spy, and sells the information or keeps it to pretend to be you.|
|adware| gives you pop up ads on web browsers, often bundled with spyware.|
|backdoor| gains unauthorized access by bypassing security measures, can result in remote access given to hackers.|
|Ransomware| holds your system for ransom, refusing access. often spread through phishing emails.|
|scareware| trys to scare you into doing something that will ultimately make everything worse. telling you your system is at risk, and trying to convince you to take action against it, leading to more issues.| 
|rootkit| modifies system os to make detection even harder to detect. also more often than not, creates a backdoor, allowing for more access.|
|viruses| attaches itself to a file, and replicates, like cancer. they can do next to nothing or completely remove data. viruses can also mutate to avoid detection.|
|trojan horse| makes itself seem perfectly fine and sneaks malicious code onto your system, often transmitted through image files, games, or audio files.|
|worm| like a virus, it replicates itself to infect further, unlike a virus, it can exist on its own and seeks out other systems to infect instead of its host system.|

Social engineering is a way of making a person beleieve that they should do what they say. by making the victim believ they are someone important or helpful, and making them download something malicious.

Denial of service attacks interrput traffic to a webpage either by flooding the site with traffic or sending bad packages. 

DDOS is similar except it is a combined effort against once source, ultimately causing even more issues.

botnets are a collection of infected non-human controlled systems that can be used as multiple hacking groups when in actuality it is just a collection of computers not even being touched. 

Man in the middle attacks are an example of on-path attacks, with man in the middle the man in the middle intercepts data being transfered before it can get to its intended destination and captures it before it can be sent through. 
Man in the Mobile is another form of an on-path attack. These attacks capture use of an infected mobile device and captures two factor authentication codes to use to gain access.

SEO (search engine optimizing) posioning is when malicious sites are better optimized to be pushed higher on webpage browsers like google by using key words often searched.

Wifi passwords cracking can be done by brute force attacks, shoulder surfing, or social engineering to gain access to a private network so hackers can impliment malicious software more easilly.
