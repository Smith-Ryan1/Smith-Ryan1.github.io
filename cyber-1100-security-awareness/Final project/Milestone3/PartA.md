Device code phising is a phising attack that is designed to trick a user into giving a threat actor access to MFA protected services by asking a user to sign into productivity apps, such as say for example, Legion, a app that does scheduling, that user then clicks the link that is sent to them by the bad actor, logging into the "service", the bad actor then uses a man-in-the-middle attack to capture the MFA code, should one be needed, and now has access to the service. 

A social engineering group, Storm-2372, uses this trick to then capture emails from users after recieving their credentials and uses the captured emails to search for more credentials that they can use to compromise other accounts. They target prominant members of government, tellecomm services, and IT service providers across several continents.

So, how does this affect Home systems? A number of ways actually. Say this happens to you, your email gets hacked through this social engineering hack. Then they gain access to login credentials through your email to your home system, now they have access to your locks, your cameras, your life. They sell your feed to criminals who capture more data, who can control your home.
