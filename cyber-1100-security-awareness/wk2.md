This week we have learned about personal security threats such as falling for phishing scams and unsafe password practices. Phishing scams are one of the most common personal cyberdecurity risks, and also one of the easiest to defend against. Most phising attacks aim toward immediant, irrational fear, the best thing you can do is stop, breath, and assess the situation before making any rash judgement calls. Sometimes Phishers will pretend to be someone important, like a boss, and make demands, or, unfortunately, use ai generated mockups of a childs voice, making parents fear for their saftey. Passwords are also prone to compromise as repeated, short, or generic passwords are often easiest to crack and provide the most access. To keep passwords secure, keep them complex, and keep them unique. It also helps to change your password every so often to make sure they are secure and people do not gain access to your accounts.
