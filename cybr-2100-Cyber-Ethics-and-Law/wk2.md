There is a subtle, yet key difference between notice and consent. Notice being the premonishion to concent, it tells you exactly what it is that you are signing up for. Tells you what will be done, and for how long. Consent is your knowing agreement to the aforementioned notice. 
Purpose limitation is, just that, you are limiting the purpose of data collection, or whatever it is you are doing.
Data minimization is similar to purpose limitation in that, you are stating what limited data you are collecting. 
Proportionality is having the proper scope of monitoring for the proper risk associated to the data. You arn't going to comb through childrens laptops and tablets for terrorist threats, though you might minimize data collection to keep their identification secure.
